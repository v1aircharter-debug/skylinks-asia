Unbroken Cold Chain Logistics

Protecting Efficacy, Ensuring Freshness

Temperature excursions cost pharmaceutical companies millions and can cost lives. We source cargo aircraft capable of supporting active cooling containers (like Envirotainer and CSafe) and provide expedited ground handling to minimize tarmac exposure. From mRNA vaccines requiring deep freeze to fresh tropical fruit, we maintain the cold chain from origin to destination.

Use Cases

Specialized Cold Chain Solutions

Vaccines & Life-Science Pharma

We arrange WHO-compliant logistics for clinical trials, biologics, and global vaccine distribution. Our charter desk coordinates dry ice replenishment and active container leasing for strict +2°C to +8°C or deep-freeze requirements.

Fresh Produce & Perishables

Maximize shelf life and minimize spoilage. We provide dedicated freighter charter for cut flowers, fresh seafood, and exotic fruits, ensuring rapid transit and ventilated temperature-controlled environments.

Active Cool Container Logistics

Need an active container (RKN or RAP) but lack scheduled flight access? We charter freighters specifically sized to carry your temperature-sensitive pallets, ensuring your container never sits idle waiting for commercial lift.
