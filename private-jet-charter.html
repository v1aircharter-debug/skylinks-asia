How It Works

From Enquiry to Wheels Up

A single coordinated process across four stages. Typical turnaround from enquiry to a returned quote: 2 to 4 hours during business hours.

1

Enquiry

Share your route, dates, passenger count, and luggage. If your plans are flexible, tell us — flexible dates unlock better aircraft and pricing.

2

Sourcing

We return 2–3 vetted operator options with aircraft type, tail registration, insurance confirmation, and a fully itemised quote — typically within 4 hours.

3

Confirmation

Sign the charter agreement, arrange payment via a business account tied to the operator or bonded broker trust, and receive a trip brief with FBO and handling details.

4

Execution

Catering loaded, FBO assigned, ground transport coordinated, permits filed. Arrive 15–20 minutes before departure; your crew handles the rest.

Frequently Asked Questions

Private Jet Charter FAQs

Private jet charter is a whole-aircraft booking on a business jet operated by a certified AOC holder. Skylinks sources the aircraft, arranges permits and ground handling, and the operator flies the mission. You pay for the aircraft, not per seat.

Block-hour pricing runs roughly $3,500 to $5,500 for a light jet, $5,000 to $8,000 for a midsize jet, and $8,000 to $15,000+ for a heavy or ultra-long-range jet. Total trip cost depends on flight hours, positioning, permits, and season. Skylinks returns a tailored quote within 24 hours.

Skylinks sources light jets (Citation, Learjet, Hawker 400XP), midsize jets (Citation Latitude, Hawker 850XP), heavy jets (Challenger 605, Falcon 900EX), and ultra-long-range jets (Gulfstream G650, Falcon 7X). The right aircraft depends on passenger count, route length, and luggage.

Yes. Empty legs are repositioning flights sold at 50 to 75 percent below standard one-way charter rates. Skylinks maintains 22 dedicated empty-leg pages across Asia and the Middle East and offers email alerts for specific routes. Availability is short-notice, usually 48 hours to 14 days out.

The operator owns or leases the aircraft, employs the crew, and holds the AOC that makes the flight legal — they are the regulated safety party. The broker arranges the booking, sources the aircraft, and manages the transaction. Skylinks is a broker: we vet operators and disclose the named AOC holder on every quote.

For a standard one-off charter, 3 to 7 days of notice is typical. For complex multi-leg missions or peak-season travel, 2 to 3 weeks is safer. Empty legs are, by definition, short-notice — usually under 2 weeks. Urgent and AOG missions can be arranged in hours depending on aircraft availability.

A standard quote includes aircraft, crew, fuel, standard catering, landing and handling fees, and overflight permits for the quoted route. It excludes de-icing, VIP catering beyond standard, ground transport, and any route changes after confirmation. Skylinks itemises the quote so nothing is hidden.

Yes. Skylinks arranges bespoke catering, FBO selection, ground transport (limousine or SUV), immigration handling, and any special requests such as pet transport, medical equipment, or sensitive cargo. These are quoted as separate line items so you can see exactly what you are paying for.

Yes — one-way charters are standard, and pricing typically includes a positioning leg to return the aircraft to its base. This is why one-way charters cost roughly 1.7 times a round-trip per-leg rate. Empty legs avoid the return positioning cost entirely and are the most cost-effective one-way option.

For international flights, passengers need a valid passport with at least 6 months' validity, appropriate visas for the destination, and any health documentation required by the destination country. Skylinks coordinates with the operator to ensure permits and immigration filings are in place. Domestic flights typically require government-issued photo ID only.
