Private Jet Charter FAQs

Choosing the right private jet depends on three factors: passenger count, required range, and luggage volume. Light jets (up to 8 pax, 1,500 nm) are ideal for short regional hops. Midsize jets (8–12 pax, 2,000–3,500 nm) offer stand-up cabins for longer transcontinental flights. Heavy jets (13+ pax, 4,000+ nm) are built for intercontinental travel. Skylinks Asia sources all three categories from certified VIP operators.

Light jets are the most economical, seating 4–8 passengers with ranges up to 1,500 nm. Midsize jets offer more cabin space, seating 8–12 with ranges of 2,000–3,500 nm. Heavy jets are the largest, offering stand-up cabins, berthing, and ranges exceeding 4,000 nm for transoceanic missions.

Charter costs depend on aircraft size, route, and region. Light jets typically cost $2,500–$4,500 per hour, midsize jets $3,800–$9,500 per hour, and heavy jets $7,000–$15,000+ per hour. Empty leg flights can reduce these costs by 25% to 75%.

Yes. As a charter broker, Skylinks Asia provides full transparency. Once we source options from our operator network, we share the exact aircraft model, year of manufacture, tail number, and interior photos before you commit to a booking.

An empty leg is a flight where an aircraft is repositioning without passengers. Instead of flying empty, operators offer these one-way flights at a significant discount—often 25% to 75% below standard charter rates. Availability is dynamic and requires flexibility.

For optimal aircraft availability and pricing, we recommend booking 2 to 4 weeks in advance. For urgent, last-minute missions (within 24–48 hours), our 24/7 charter desk can usually source an aircraft from our network, though availability may be limited.

Yes, pets are generally allowed on private jet charters, unlike commercial airlines. Specific policies depend on the operator and the aircraft. We recommend informing the charter desk at the time of enquiry so we can confirm the aircraft's pet policy and any applicable fees.

Baggage capacity varies by aircraft. Light jets typically hold 40–65 cubic feet, midsize jets 65–150 cubic feet, and heavy jets 150–250+ cubic feet. Exterior baggage compartments are accessible in flight on most midsize and heavy jets. Skylinks Asia can advise on specific volume for your group size.

Skylinks Asia exclusively sources aircraft from operators holding valid AOCs (Air Operator Certificates) and meeting stringent third-party safety audits such as ARGUS, Wyvern, or IS-BAO. We do not compromise on safety, regardless of the charter budget.
