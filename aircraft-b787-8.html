Overview

Aircraft Profile

The Boeing 787-8 Dreamliner is the foundational variant of the highly successful 787 family, engineered to revolutionize long-haul aviation. Built primarily from composite materials, it offers a 20% improvement in fuel efficiency compared to older widebodies, making it an incredibly economical choice for intercontinental group charters.

Accommodating up to 359 passengers, the 787-8 is ideal for corporate MICE events, specialized sports travel, and medium-sized diplomatic delegations. Its standout feature is passenger comfort: the composite fuselage allows for higher cabin humidity and a lower cabin altitude (6,000 feet), which drastically reduces jet lag and fatigue. Combined with the largest windows in commercial aviation and advanced mood lighting, the 787-8 ensures passengers arrive refreshed and on schedule.

Frequently Asked Questions

787-8 Charter Queries

How many passengers can the Boeing 787-8 hold?

In a typical two-class charter configuration, the Boeing 787-8 can comfortably accommodate up to 359 passengers, making it ideal for medium-to-large international groups, corporate MICE events, and sports travel.

What is the maximum range of a chartered Boeing 787-8?

The Boeing 787-8 has a maximum range of approximately 13,530 kilometers (7,305 nautical miles), allowing for up to 14 hours of continuous flight. This makes it capable of connecting major intercontinental hubs non-stop.

What makes the 787-8 Dreamliner more comfortable for passengers?

The 787 features a composite airframe that allows for higher cabin humidity and a lower cabin altitude equivalent to 6,000 feet (compared to 8,000 feet on standard airliners). This significantly reduces passenger fatigue, jet lag, and dryness. It also features the largest windows in commercial aviation and advanced mood lighting.

Can the Boeing 787-8 be configured for VIP or executive travel?

Yes, the 787-8 is a highly popular base for Boeing Business Jets (BBJ). In a VIP configuration, the aircraft can be outfitted with luxury lounges, private bedrooms, and meeting rooms to accommodate around 25 to 50 VIP passengers.

What kind of runways does the 787-8 require for charter flights?

As a commercial widebody jet, the Boeing 787-8 requires paved, standardized runways typically at least 2,500 meters (8,200 feet) in length. However, its composite landing gear and wing design allow it to operate from some regional airports that cannot accommodate larger widebodies like the 747.
