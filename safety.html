In aviation, there is no compromise. Skylinks Asia Aviation sources aircraft exclusively from operators who meet the highest international safety audits and regulatory compliance standards.

Our Standard

Audited. Verified. Certified.

As an independent charter broker, our primary value to clients is our rigorous vetting process. We act as your in-house aviation safety department. Before any aircraft is proposed, we verify the operator's Air Operator Certificate (AOC), insurance limits, crew training records, and aircraft maintenance history. If an operator does not pass our internal audit, they do not fly our clients.

The Process

How We Vet Our Operators

Third-Party Audits

We prioritize operators who actively maintain recognized industry safety certifications.

AOC Verification

We validate the operator's AOC and ensure the specific aircraft is legally registered under their certificate.

Crew Qualifications

Confirming flight crew currency, medical certificates, and specific route/aircraft experience.

Insurance Limits

Verifying adequate public liability, passenger, and cargo insurance coverage for your specific mission.

Frequently Asked Questions

Safety & Compliance Inquiries

What is an Air Operator Certificate (AOC)?

An AOC is the legal certification granted by a national aviation authority that allows an operator to carry out commercial air transport. We verify the AOC of every operator we source aircraft from to ensure full legal compliance.

Can we receive safety documentation for our flight?

Yes, corporate and government clients can request complete operator safety dossiers, including AOCs, insurance certificates, and third-party audit reports, before finalizing any charter.

Request a Safety Dossier

Corporate clients and government agencies can request complete operator safety dossiers, insurance certificates, and AOC documentation for any proposed aircraft.
