Empty Leg Flights Explained

How they work, safety, and real cost savings for private jet charter across Asia, Middle East & Africa.

Private jet travel has a reputation for being out of reach — until you understand how empty leg flights work. If you've heard the term but aren't sure whether it's safe, how much you'll actually save, or how quickly you need to act, this guide walks through everything a first-time flyer needs to know.

What Is an Empty Leg Flight? (And How It Works)

An empty leg flight is a private jet flight that operates without paying passengers — usually because the aircraft needs to reposition after dropping off a charter client, or to reach the location of its next scheduled booking.

Here's the sequence that creates one:

  1. A client books a private jet for a one-way trip — say, Dubai to Islamabad.
  2. Once that client lands, the aircraft can't simply stay there. It needs to return to its home base, or fly to wherever its next confirmed charter begins.
  3. That repositioning flight would normally fly with an empty cabin — a cost the operator absorbs either way, since fuel and crew are paid regardless of passengers on board.
  4. Instead of flying it empty, the operator lists the seat (or the whole aircraft) at a steep discount, recovering some of that cost while giving travelers a rare shot at private aviation for a fraction of the usual price.

You'll come across a few other names for the same thing as you research this online — deadhead flight, repositioning flight, and ferry flight all describe the same concept. If you're new to private jet travel, the important thing to understand is this: an empty leg isn't a different, lesser class of private jet flight. It's the same aircraft, the same crew, and the same in-flight experience as a full-price charter — you're simply catching it on a leg it was already going to fly.

Is Booking an Empty Leg Flight Safe?

Yes — and this is one of the most common hesitations first-time flyers have, so it's worth addressing directly. An empty leg flight uses the exact same aircraft, the same certified crew, and operates under the exact same safety standards as a full-price charter on that route. The discount reflects repositioning economics, not a shortcut on safety.

That said, as with any private charter booking, it's reasonable to verify who you're flying with. Before booking, you can check for:

What to verify before you book (quick checklist)

  • The operator holds a valid air operator certificate for commercial charter flights
  • The operator carries an internationally recognized safety audit or rating (the kind of independent aviation safety audits used industry-wide)
  • You're booking through a broker or operator who discloses the operating airline clearly, rather than an anonymous listing

When you book through Skylinks Asia, our team works only with vetted operators across our network, so this verification is already built into the process — but it's still good practice to ask questions and understand who is actually operating your flight.

Empty Leg vs. Full Private Jet Charter: Cost Comparison

The appeal of an empty leg comes down to one thing: price. Across the industry, empty leg flights typically list at 25% to 75% below the equivalent full charter rate for the same aircraft and route.

Full Private Charter Empty Leg Flight
Aircraft & crew Booked specifically for you Same aircraft, already flying that route
Route & timing Fully flexible — you choose Fixed — set by the original charter's schedule
Typical price 100% of standard charter rate Roughly 25%–75% below standard rate
Booking window Can be booked well in advance Usually last-minute

Worked example: A one-way charter from Dubai to Islamabad on a midsize jet might run at full standard charter rates when booked directly. If that same aircraft needs to reposition back to Dubai — or onward to its next charter — after dropping off a client, that repositioning sector could be listed as an empty leg at a significant discount off the standard one-way rate. The aircraft, the crew, and the in-flight experience are identical; what you're trading for the lower price is control over the exact date and time.

The trade-off is straightforward: you get private jet travel at a meaningfully lower price, in exchange for giving up control over the exact route, date, and departure time. If your travel plans are flexible, that trade-off works heavily in your favor.

How Last-Minute Can You Book an Empty Leg Flight?

This is the part first-time flyers are least prepared for: empty leg flights move fast. Most are listed somewhere between 48 and 72 hours before departure, and some appear same-day, once an operator confirms the original charter and knows exactly when and where the aircraft needs to reposition.

That's also why availability is never guaranteed in advance and why flexibility is your biggest advantage. A traveler who can move on short notice, or who has some give in their travel dates, has a far better chance of matching an open empty leg than someone locked into a single fixed date.

Empty leg flights are highly sought after and strictly subject to prior sale. We recommend requesting your preferred flight immediately to secure availability. FAQ

What is an empty leg flight?

An empty leg flight is a private jet flight — usually a repositioning flight after a one-way charter — that operates without paying passengers unless it's sold at a discount to fill the seats.

Are empty leg flights safe?

Yes. Empty leg flights use the same aircraft, crew, and safety standards as a full-price private jet charter on the same route. The discount reflects repositioning economics, not reduced safety.

How much cheaper is an empty leg flight than a full charter?

Empty leg flights are typically listed at 25% to 75% below the standard charter rate for the same aircraft and route, though the exact discount varies by route, aircraft, and timing.

How far in advance are empty leg flights listed?

Most empty leg flights are listed 48 to 72 hours before departure, with some appearing same-day. They are rarely available far in advance, since they depend on the schedule of the original charter booking.

How do I get notified about empty leg flights on my route?

Register your frequent departure and destination cities with Skylinks Asia, and our concierge team will contact you directly by WhatsApp or email the moment a matching empty leg becomes available.

The Bottom Line

Empty leg flights give you the same private jet experience as a full charter — the same aircraft, the same crew, the same standard of service — at a fraction of the cost, in exchange for flexibility on timing. For travelers across Europe, Asia, Africa, and the Middle East who can move with short notice, they're one of the smartest ways to fly private.
