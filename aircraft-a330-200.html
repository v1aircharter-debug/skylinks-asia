Aircraft Overview

Efficiency Meets Long-Haul Comfort

The Airbus A330-200 is a highly versatile widebody passenger aircraft designed to meet the demands of modern long-haul group travel. Built on the success of the A330 family, it offers a perfect balance of extended range, passenger comfort, and operating economics. With the ability to carry up to 280 passengers over 13,450 kilometers, the A330-200 is ideal for international corporate MICE events, diplomatic delegations, and large sports team transport. Its twin-aisle cabin provides a spacious and comfortable environment, reducing boarding times and ensuring a premium travel experience. Additionally, the aircraft is a popular choice for VIP conversion (ACJ), offering luxury lounges and private suites for heads of state and executive teams.

Frequently Asked Questions

A330-200 Charter Inquiries

How many passengers can the Airbus A330-200 hold?

In a typical two-class charter configuration, the Airbus A330-200 can comfortably accommodate up to 280 passengers, making it ideal for large international groups, sports teams, and corporate MICE events.

What is the maximum range of a chartered Airbus A330-200?

The A330-200 has a maximum range of approximately 13,450 kilometers (7,250 nautical miles), allowing for up to 14 hours of continuous flight. This makes it capable of connecting major intercontinental hubs non-stop.

Can the Airbus A330-200 be configured for VIP or executive travel?

Yes, the A330-200 is frequently converted into an Airbus Corporate Jet (ACJ). In a VIP configuration, the aircraft can be outfitted with luxury lounges, private bedrooms, and meeting rooms to accommodate around 20 to 50 VIP passengers.

What kind of runways does the A330-200 require for charter flights?

As a commercial widebody jet, the Airbus A330-200 requires paved, standardized runways typically at least 2,500 meters (8,200 feet) in length. It is designed for major international airports rather than regional or unpaved strips.

How much baggage can the Airbus A330-200 carry?

The A330-200 offers generous belly cargo space in addition to large overhead bins inside the cabin. For a full flight of 280 passengers, it can easily accommodate standard checked luggage. For groups with excess baggage or heavy equipment, the cargo hold can be configured to prioritize freight.
