Overview

Aircraft Profile

The Antonov An-124 "Ruslan" is a strategic airlift jet designed to transport oversized and ultra-heavy cargo that no other commercial aircraft can accommodate. With a maximum payload capacity of 150 tonnes, the An-124 is the backbone of global project logistics, frequently called upon for humanitarian relief, military transport, and aerospace logistics.

One of the defining features of the An-124 is its unique cargo handling system. The aircraft is equipped with a nose cargo door that opens upward, allowing for straight-in loading of massive items. Additionally, the aircraft features a "kneeling" capability—where the landing gear retracts to lower the fuselage to ground level—making the loading of heavy rolling stock and pallets significantly easier without specialized ground infrastructure.

Frequently Asked Questions

An-124 Ruslan Charter Queries

What is the maximum payload capacity of the Antonov An-124 Ruslan?

The Antonov An-124 has a maximum payload capacity of approximately 150,000 kg (150 metric tons or 330,000 pounds), making it one of the heaviest lift cargo aircraft in the world.

How does the An-124 load its cargo?

The An-124 features both a front and rear cargo door. The nose can open upward to allow straight-in loading of outsized vehicles and machinery. It also has a "kneeling" capability where the landing gear retracts to lower the fuselage to ground level for easier loading.

What type of cargo is typically transported on the An-124?

The An-124 is used for outsized and ultra-heavy cargo such as aircraft engines, industrial turbines, oil and gas drilling equipment, mining machinery, railway locomotives, and large humanitarian relief supplies.

What is the range of the Antonov An-124?

At maximum payload (150 tons), the An-124 has a range of approximately 4,800 kilometers (2,600 nautical miles). With lighter payloads, it can fly significantly further, allowing for global strategic airlift with technical stops.
