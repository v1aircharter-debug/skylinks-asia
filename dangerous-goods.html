Certified Hazmat Transport

Strict Compliance. Absolute Safety.

Transporting hazardous materials by air requires rigorous compliance with IATA Dangerous Goods Regulations (DGR). We work only with certified cargo operators and ground handlers who are explicitly approved to carry specific DG classes. From Class 1 explosives for the mining sector to Class 9 lithium batteries for EV manufacturers, we ensure your cargo is documented, packed, and loaded with zero compromises on safety. If you need rapid transport for these critical goods, see our urgent cargo charter solutions.

Use Cases

Specialized DGR Charter Solutions

Flammable Liquids & Chemicals

Class 3 flammable liquids, Class 8 corrosives, and Class 5 oxidizers. We arrange dedicated cargo flights for chemical manufacturers moving industrial solvents, resins, and acids globally, complementing our oil and gas equipment transport services.

Lithium Batteries & Tech Cargo

Class 9 lithium-ion batteries pose severe fire risks on passenger aircraft. We source freighters for EV manufacturers and tech firms to transport bulk batteries safely and compliantly.

Explosives & Munitions (Class 1)

Moving detonators, blasting caps, or mining explosives requires specialized security and segregation. We coordinate highly secure charter flights for defense and mining logistics.
