Aircraft Overview

Unmatched Narrowbody Payload

The Boeing 757-200 Freighter (757-200F) is a highly capable and versatile narrowbody cargo aircraft. Designed with cargo operators in mind, it offers the highest payload capacity of any narrowbody freighter, effectively bridging the gap between smaller regional jets and massive widebody freighters.

With its impressive 15-pallet main deck capacity and massive 36,290 kg payload, the 757-200F is a favorite for heavy express freight, e-commerce logistics, and even live animal transport due to its superior environmental control systems. Its ability to operate efficiently into regional airports while carrying heavy, dense cargo makes it an indispensable asset for global supply chains.

Frequently Asked Questions

757-200F Charter Inquiries

What is the maximum payload capacity of the Boeing 757-200 Freighter?

The Boeing 757-200 Freighter has a maximum payload capacity of approximately 36,290 kg (39.9 metric tons or 80,000 pounds), offering the highest payload capacity in the narrowbody freighter class.

How many pallets can the 757-200F carry on its main deck?

The main deck of the 757-200F can accommodate up to 15 standard AAA pallets (measuring 88 inches by 125 inches), in addition to bulk cargo in the lower belly.

What is the maximum range of the Boeing 757-200F freighter?

At maximum payload, the Boeing 757-200F has a range of approximately 5,800 kilometers (3,900 nautical miles), making it capable of flying transcontinental and regional logistics routes.

What types of cargo is the B757-200F best suited for?

It is highly versatile and commonly used for heavy express freight, e-commerce logistics, automotive parts, and even live animals due to its excellent environmental control systems and containerized cargo capacity.

Why is the 757-200F popular for regional cargo networks?

The 757-200F is popular because it combines narrowbody operating economics with a payload capacity that rivals some widebody freighters. Its ability to operate into smaller regional airports while carrying heavy loads makes it a favorite for integrators.
