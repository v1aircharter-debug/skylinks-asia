The Ultimate Guide to Planning a
Hajj & Umrah Charter Flight

GACA approvals, slot allocation, aircraft selection, baggage allowances, and group logistics — everything a pilgrimage tour operator needs for the 2027 season.

Quick answer: Planning a Hajj or Umrah charter flight for 2027 requires early commitment, GACA approval through the correct channels, and the right widebody aircraft in a high-density pilgrim configuration. Organizing group travel for Hajj and Umrah requires strict adherence to schedules, comfort for the elderly, and capacity for baggage. Commercial flights often lack the space, timing flexibility, and group coordination required for such a massive endeavor. Chartered flights offer an unmatched alternative, allowing operators to dictate departure times, choose specific aircraft configurations, and ensure the group remains together.

Why Choose a Chartered Flight for Hajj and Umrah?

The spiritual significance of Hajj and Umrah cannot be overstated. However, the physical toll of international travel can detract from the experience. By chartering a dedicated aircraft through a specialist Hajj & Umrah charter service, operators provide their groups with several critical advantages:

  • Exclusive group cohesion. Keep your entire congregation together from departure to arrival. No risk of group members separated onto different commercial flights or left behind due to overbooking.
  • Flexible scheduling. Commercial airlines operate on their timetables. A chartered flight lets you select departure and arrival times matching your group's hotel check-in, transport connections, and ritual schedules in Mecca and Medina.
  • Generous baggage allowance. Chartered flights offer customizable baggage solutions — typically 40kg to 46kg per passenger — far exceeding standard commercial economy limits. Special arrangements can also be made for Zamzam water on the return flight.
  • Dedicated ground handling. Charter brokers coordinate with dedicated handlers at Jeddah (JED) or Madinah (MED). This ensures fast-track immigration, dedicated baggage carousels, and immediate bus transfers to hotels, bypassing commercial terminal congestion.
  • Elderly-friendly service. Wheelchair assistance, soft-textured catering, and cabin temperature control for passengers in Ihram can all be arranged on a charter — impossible on a shared commercial rotation.

What GACA Approvals Are Required for a Hajj Charter?

Operating a Hajj or Umrah charter into Saudi Arabia requires a specific set of GACA approvals. The process involves three linked workstreams:

1. Foreign Air Carrier License (Hajj and Umrah)

The operator must apply for a Foreign Air Carrier License specifically for Hajj and Umrah charter operations. GACA reviews all submitted documents and may request updates before issuing approval.

2. Flight Schedule Approval

All Hajj flight requests, including the flight schedule, must be submitted to GACA through the designated air carrier under bilateral agreement provisions. Applications are sent to achq@gaca.gov.sa with a copy to hud@gaca.gov.sa.

3. Landing and Overflight Permits

Landing and overflight permits are processed through GACA. Slot requests for Hajj season are submitted through the same channel. Required documents include:

  • Valid economic license or approval for scheduled/charter operation
  • Foreign Operator Authority (FOA)
  • Acceptance of Security Program (AOSP)
  • Bank Guarantee (Housing and Catering / Return of Pilgrims Expenses)
  • HUD-101 Form
  • Flight Scheduling Passenger Security System Compliance Certificate (APP/PNR)

Key compliance requirements

  • GACA approval must be obtained prior to carrying pilgrims from a third country to Saudi Arabia.
  • Passengers cannot be re-booked on other airlines without prior GACA approval.
  • Air carriers must commit to carrying pilgrims back on their flights, especially if flights are postponed due to airspace closures.
  • Any pilgrims arriving with expired, forged, or non-existent passports or Hajj visas must be denied boarding.

How Are Jeddah and Madinah Slots Allocated?

Slot allocation is the single biggest constraint on Hajj charter planning. Jeddah and Madinah operate very different systems.

Airport Slot Authority Submission Method
Jeddah (OEJN)Airport Coordination Limited (ACL)Coordinated centrally with strict regulatory requirements at the dedicated Hajj Terminal
Madinah (OEMA)Tibah Airports / Prince Mohammad bin Abdulaziz IntlStandard SCR message to SITA address MEDPAXH, or email medslotteam@tibahairports.com

Turn times that run 45 minutes in a normal week routinely stretch to 90–120 minutes in Hajj week due to pilgrim processing, Zamzam water loading, and baggage volumes. Build this into the rotation plan rather than assume baseline. Parking is limited during peak Hajj weeks — operators should confirm parking availability at the time of slot request.

What this means in practice: If your flight isn't slotted into Jeddah's Hajj Terminal before the window closes, you will not fly. Late applications rarely find space. This is why charter brokers recommend committing aircraft and filing slot requests as soon as the GACA application window opens.

Selecting the Right Aircraft for Pilgrimage Charters

Choosing the right aircraft is critical for balancing cost and comfort. The aircraft must be capable of long-haul flights while accommodating large groups in high-density economy configurations. Through our operator network, we source licensed AOC holders providing the most suitable widebody and narrowbody aircraft for Asian, Middle Eastern, and African markets. See our private jet fleet page for the smaller VIP pilgrimage option.

Wide-Body Aircraft (300 – 490+ Seats)

For large groups traveling from Asia or Africa, aircraft like the Boeing 777-300ER (up to 490 seats in high-density configuration), Airbus A330-300, Boeing 747-400 (500+ seats), and Airbus A340-300 (295 seats) are ideal. They offer the range and capacity to move entire congregations in a single wave.

Narrow-Body Aircraft (150 – 200 Seats)

For medium-sized groups or shorter regional Umrah routes, the Boeing 737-800 or Airbus A320 provide highly cost-effective solutions without sacrificing reliability. These are commonly used for year-round Umrah services where group sizes don't justify a widebody.

Step-by-Step Planning Guide for Hajj 2027 Operators

A successful pilgrimage charter requires months of preparation. Here is how we recommend operators approach the 2027 season:

  1. Confirm your quota and registration. Saudi Arabia allocates Hajj visas on a quota of approximately 1 pilgrim per 1,000 Muslims in each country. Registration for Hajj 2027 closed 26 September 2026. If you missed it, focus on Ramadan Umrah 2027 (February) and Hajj 2028 planning.
  2. Secure aircraft early. GACA allocates specific slots for Hajj and Umrah charters. For Hajj 2027, aircraft should already be committed. For Ramadan Umrah 2027, book by autumn 2026 as demand is highest for the last ten nights.
  3. Determine passenger count and baggage. Provide exact numbers. This dictates aircraft size. Be transparent about baggage expectations (especially Zamzam water on the return leg) to ensure the aircraft is not weight-restricted. Charter flights typically allow 40–46kg per passenger.
  4. Coordinate visas and documentation. Ensure all passengers have valid passports and Hajj/Umrah visas. Pilgrims' information must be uploaded to the Masar Nusuk system between 14 August 2026 and 28 January 2027. The passenger manifest — including name as per passport, passport number, visa number, and group leader code — must follow GACA format (Arabic and Latin spelling).
  5. Plan catering and special needs. Charter flights allow you to dictate the catering menu. Halal meals are standard, and elderly-friendly menus — soft rice, low-spice dishes, easily digestible proteins — can be requested. Wheelchairs and mobility assistance can be arranged seamlessly. Cabin temperature is maintained for passengers in Ihram.
  6. Coordinate ground transport in KSA. Do not let passengers wait at the airport. Coordinate buses at JED or MED to transfer the group directly to their hotels as soon as the aircraft lands. Fast-track immigration and dedicated baggage carousels at the Hajj Terminal make this far smoother than commercial arrivals.
  7. Confirm return logistics. Zamzam water is strictly prohibited inside personal luggage. Pilgrims flying on approved carriers receive a sealed 5-litre bottle at their home airport on arrival. Confirm this arrangement with the carrier before departure.

Quotas directly influence charter demand. A country with a large quota and limited scheduled capacity sees the highest demand for charter lift. This is where early aircraft commitment matters most.
