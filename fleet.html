Frequently Asked Questions

Charter Fleet Inquiries

Do you own the aircraft listed on your fleet page?

No, Skylinks Asia Aviation is an independent charter broker. We do not own aircraft, which allows us to objectively source the best available aircraft from our global network of certified operators based on your specific payload and route requirements.

Can I charter a freighter aircraft for just one piece of oversized cargo?

Yes, we arrange dedicated freighter charters for single pieces of oversized cargo (like oil & gas equipment or vehicles) as well as full consolidated cargo loads. We source the exact aircraft needed, from ATR 72s to Boeing 747Fs.

Don't See Your Required Aircraft?

Our network extends far beyond this list. If you need a specific VIP configuration, an Antonov for outsize cargo, or a DC-8 for heavy freight, we can source it for you.
