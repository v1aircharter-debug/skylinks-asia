Skylinks Asia Aviation (“we”, “us”, “our”) is committed to protecting the privacy and security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and disclose your data when you use our website or engage our aircraft charter brokerage services. We comply with all applicable data protection laws, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Information We Collect

We collect information necessary to provide you with accurate charter quotes and to fulfill our brokerage obligations. This includes:

1.1 Contact Information

Name, email address, phone number, and company/organization name.

1.2 Flight & Cargo Details

Departure and destination airports, preferred dates, passenger counts, cargo weights, dimensions, and specific handling requirements (e.g., Dangerous Goods declarations).

1.3 Billing Information

Bank details, billing addresses, and transaction records required for invoicing and accounting purposes.

2. How We Use Your Data

We process your personal data strictly for the following purposes:

  • To source aircraft options and provide you with charter quotes.
  • To communicate with you regarding your inquiry, flight status, or active charter.
  • To coordinate with third-party aircraft operators, ground handlers, and permit authorities to execute your flight.
  • To process payments and maintain financial records.
  • To send you occasional industry insights and updates (you may opt out at any time).

3. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell your personal data. However, to operate your charter flight, we must share specific details with trusted third parties. This includes:

  • Aircraft Operators: To secure the aircraft, we share passenger manifests, cargo details, and operational requirements.
  • Aviation Authorities: To secure overflight and landing permits, passenger information may be transmitted to civil aviation bodies.
  • Ground Handlers: To arrange catering, fuel, and cargo loading at origin and destination airports.

We may also disclose data if legally compelled to do so by law enforcement or government agencies.

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration. Access to your personal data is restricted to authorized personnel who require it to perform their job functions. However, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Your Data Rights (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have certain data protection rights:

  • Right to Access: You can request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: You can request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to Erasure: You can request the deletion of your data when it is no longer necessary for the purpose for which it was collected.
  • Right to Object: You can object to the processing of your data for direct marketing.

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the details below.

6. Cookies

Our website uses minimal cookies to ensure basic functionality and to analyze website traffic. We do not use cookies for intrusive advertising. You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings.
