Why ACMI Wet Leasing Solves Seasonal Demand

August 2024•Skylinks Asia Team

Airlines facing seasonal demand spikes often face a dilemma: buy new aircraft (which is costly and inflexible) or turn away profitable passengers. ACMI wet leasing provides a turnkey solution, offering immediate capacity exactly when needed.

What is ACMI Wet Leasing?

ACMI stands for Aircraft, Crew, Maintenance, and Insurance. In a wet lease, the lessor provides the aircraft along with the flight crew, cabin crew, maintenance, and insurance. The lessee (the airline) simply pays for fuel, airport fees, and passenger handling.

ACMI vs Dry Lease: Which is Right?

A dry lease provides only the aircraft, requiring the lessee to use their own AOC and crew. This is suited for long-term expansion. ACMI, however, is ideal for short-term needs like Hajj season, summer peaks, or AOG (Aircraft on Ground) situations where immediate capacity is required.

Benefits of ACMI for Seasonal Demand

ACMI allows airlines to scale their fleet up or down within weeks, avoiding the financial burden of idle aircraft during off-peak seasons. It also allows carriers to test new routes without long-term risk.
