Overview

Aircraft Profile

The Airbus A320 family is the cornerstone of modern short-to-medium-haul aviation. For passenger operations, the A320 offers a spacious, comfortable cabin capable of seating 180 passengers, making it the preferred choice for corporate MICE events, sports team transport, and regional group travel.

For cargo operators, the A320P2F (Passenger-to-Freighter) conversion gives this airframe a second life as a highly efficient narrowbody freighter. With a main deck capacity of 14 standard pallets and a payload of 27 metric tons, the A320P2F is directly positioned as a modern, fuel-efficient replacement for aging 737-400F fleets. It is optimized for high-density e-commerce logistics, express parcel delivery, and regional supply chain integration.

Frequently Asked Questions

A320 & A320P2F Charter Queries

What is the difference between the A320 and the A320P2F?

The Airbus A320 is the standard passenger jet configured for 180 travelers. The A320P2F (Passenger-to-Freighter) is a converted cargo aircraft where the passenger cabin is modified to accommodate 14 main deck cargo pallets, making it a dedicated freighter for e-commerce and express logistics.

How many passengers can the Airbus A320 hold?

In a typical single-class charter configuration, the Airbus A320 can comfortably accommodate up to 180 passengers, making it ideal for medium-haul group travel, sports teams, and corporate MICE events.

What is the maximum payload capacity of the A320P2F freighter?

The A320P2F has a maximum payload capacity of approximately 27,000 kg (27 metric tons). Its main deck can accommodate 14 standard AAA pallets, plus additional bulk cargo in the lower belly.

What is the range of the Airbus A320?

The passenger A320 has a maximum range of approximately 6,100 kilometers (3,300 nautical miles). The A320P2F freighter has a range of approximately 3,700 kilometers (2,000 nautical miles) at maximum payload.

Why is the A320P2F popular for e-commerce logistics?

The A320P2F offers an excellent balance of payload capacity and operating economics for short to medium-haul routes. Its containerized main deck allows for rapid loading of express freight, making it a direct, highly efficient replacement for older 737-400F freighters.
