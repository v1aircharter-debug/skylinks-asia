Aircraft Profile

Bombardier Learjet 45XR

Introduced in 2004, the Learjet 45XR is an upgraded variant of the foundational Learjet 45, which was the first entirely new Learjet design since the 1960s. By equipping the airframe with Honeywell TFE 731-20BR engines, Bombardier increased thrust and maximum takeoff weights, significantly improving performance in hot and high-altitude conditions. With over 200 units produced before manufacturing ended in 2012, the 45XR became a backbone of European and regional charter fleets. Its optimized range and comfortable cabin make it perfectly suited for frequent regional shuttles, easily connecting major financial centers and remote regional airports alike.
