Light Jet Charter FAQs

A light jet is a category of private aircraft typically seating 4 to 8 passengers with a maximum range of 1,500 to 2,000 nautical miles and cruise speeds of 400 to 450 knots. Light jets are the entry point into private aviation and are purpose-built for regional missions of 1 to 3 hours.

Most light jets have a maximum range of 1,500 to 2,000 nautical miles. The Embraer Phenom 300 leads the category at 2,077 nm, followed by the Citation CJ3 at 2,040 nm and the Learjet 45XR at 2,301 nm. Range depends on payload, altitude, wind, and fuel reserves.

Midsize jets offer a larger cabin, more baggage capacity, and a longer range than light jets, typically 2,000 to 3,000 miles. Light jets are more economical to operate and can access shorter runways and smaller airports. Midsize jets are better for longer trips or larger groups; light jets are ideal for regional missions.

Charter rates for light jets typically range from $2,800 to $6,500 per hour, depending on the specific aircraft, route, region, and season. The Citation V sits at the lower end ($2,800–$4,200), while the Phenom 300 commands a premium ($4,000–$6,000). Empty leg availability can reduce costs significantly.

In the Skylinks Asia light jet fleet, the Bombardier Learjet 45XR has the longest range at 2,301 nautical miles, followed by the Embraer Phenom 300 at 2,077 nm and the Citation CJ3 at 2,040 nm. These aircraft can connect most regional city pairs non-stop with full passengers.

Yes. Most light jets are designed to operate from runways of 3,000 to 4,000 feet. The Citation V requires approximately 3,160 feet for takeoff, the Citation CJ3 around 3,180 feet, and the Phenom 300 about 3,138 feet. This allows access to hundreds of regional and secondary airports.

Light jets typically seat 4 to 8 passengers, depending on the specific model. The Citation V and Citation CJ3 are configured for 7 passengers; the Hawker 400XP, Citation Ultra, and Phenom 300 for 7 to 8; and the Learjet 45XR for up to 9. Cabin layouts vary by operator.

Yes. Light jets are the most common aircraft category on the empty leg market. Typical light jet empty legs cost $5,000 to $15,000 per leg for 1 to 3 hour trips, representing savings of 25% to 75% off full charter rates. Aircraft such as the Citation Ultra and Citation V are particularly frequent on empty leg routes.

The best light jet depends on the mission. For maximum range and cabin comfort, the Embraer Phenom 300 is the market leader. For the lowest operating cost on short hops, the Citation CJ3 and Citation V are excellent choices. For hot-and-high performance, the Learjet 45XR is preferred. For short-runway access, the Citation V's straight-wing design excels.
