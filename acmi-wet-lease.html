Turnkey Aircraft Leasing

Immediate Capacity for Airlines & Operators

When your fleet is stretched to the limit, securing backup capacity must be fast and compliant. We connect airlines with certified operators offering ACMI wet leases—providing the aircraft, full cockpit and cabin crew, maintenance, and insurance in one turnkey package. Whether you need to cover a technical defect for a week or require a narrowbody for a three-month summer surge, we negotiate the terms and handle the regulatory compliance.

Use Cases

Specialized Airline Leasing Solutions

AOG & Maintenance Cover

An unexpected technical defect can cripple your network. We source identical or equivalent aircraft types to operate your schedule while your aircraft is grounded, minimizing passenger disruption. For broken supply chains, we also arrange urgent cargo charter flights.

Seasonal Demand Surges

Capitalize on peak travel seasons without committing to long-term fleet expansion. We arrange medium to long-term wet leases to help you meet summer, Eid, or Hajj capacity demands.

New Route Launches

Test new markets risk-free. Airlines can utilize wet lease arrangements to prove a route's viability before committing to purchasing new aircraft or undertaking dry lease commitments.
