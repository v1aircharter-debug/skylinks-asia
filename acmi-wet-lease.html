Aviation Leasing

ACMI / Wet Lease

Flexible aircraft leasing for seasonal demand, fleet expansion, and urgent capacity requirements across Asia, Middle East, and Africa.

What is ACMI?

ACMI stands for Aircraft, Crew, Maintenance, and Insurance. It is a wet lease arrangement where the lessor provides the aircraft, a full flight crew, maintenance, and insurance. The lessee pays for the aircraft by the hour and covers fuel, airport fees, and other operational costs.

Why Choose ACMI?

  • Seasonal Demand: Quickly add capacity during peak seasons without long-term fleet commitments.
  • Urgent Capacity: Address sudden operational needs or aircraft grounding without delays.
  • Market Testing: Launch new routes with minimal risk before committing to aircraft acquisition.
