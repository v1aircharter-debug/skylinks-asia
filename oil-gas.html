Rig Moves & Heavy Air Freight

Keeping the Energy Sector Running

Energy infrastructure is often located in remote, harsh environments with limited ground infrastructure. Whether you are executing a planned rig move, replacing a critical refinery valve, or transporting oversized drilling machinery, we source the right freighter aircraft for the job. From ramp-loading turboprops for desert airstrips to widebody freighters for turbine engines, we ensure your energy cargo arrives safely and on schedule.

Use Cases

Specialized Energy Charter Solutions

Rig Moves & Heavy Machinery

Transporting drill pipes, mud pumps, and drilling rigs requires exact loading calculations. We charter ramp-loading aircraft (like the AN-12 or ATR 72F) to move heavy machinery directly to remote oilfields.

Pipeline Infrastructure & Compressors

Keep pipeline construction on track. We arrange dedicated cargo flights for large-diameter pipe segments, industrial compressors, and specialized welding equipment to bypass broken ground supply chains.

Refinery Turnaround & Shutdowns

Plant shutdowns operate on strict deadlines. We provide Just-In-Time (JIT) air charter for replacement valves, heat exchangers, and catalysts to prevent extended facility downtime and massive revenue loss.
