The Network

Why Jeddah is a Premier Origin Hub

Jeddah serves as the commercial capital of Saudi Arabia and the principal air gateway for Hajj and Umrah pilgrims arriving into the Kingdom. This dual role generates constant, two-way repositioning traffic. Operators dropping passengers in Jeddah frequently need to reposition empty to Riyadh, Dubai, or international leisure destinations.

Beyond domestic and intra-Gulf traffic, Jeddah's seasonal pilgrim overflow creates unique international empty leg opportunities. Aircraft repositioning from Europe and Türkiye to Jeddah for pilgrim groups often return empty, offering discounted flights back to Istanbul, Antalya, London, and the Maldives.
