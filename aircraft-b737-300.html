Overview

Aircraft Profile

The Boeing 737-300 Freighter is a highly reliable, narrow-body cargo aircraft converted from the proven 737-300 Classic passenger jet. Designed specifically to meet the demands of regional logistics, this freighter offers an exceptional balance of payload capacity and operating economics. It is a preferred choice for express freight operators moving standard pallets and bulk cargo across short and medium-haul routes.

Equipped with a large main deck cargo door and an efficient cargo handling system, the B737-300F ensures rapid turnaround times, which is critical for time-sensitive supply chains. At Skylinks Asia, we utilize this aircraft to provide agile, cost-effective, and dependable charter solutions for regional distribution and urgent freight forwarding.

Key Features

Why Charter the B737-300F?

Optimized Payload

Capable of carrying up to 16.5 metric tons of cargo, providing excellent capacity for a narrow-body regional freighter.

Cost-Effective Operations

Offers lower fuel burn and operating costs compared to larger wide-body freighters, maximizing ROI on regional routes.

Main Deck Cargo Door

Features a spacious side cargo door designed for the swift loading and unloading of standard industrial pallets.

Roller Floor System

Equipped with an integrated roller system beneath the main deck, enabling effortless maneuvering of heavy cargo pallets.

Versatile Capacity

Proven Reliability

Built on the robust 737 Classic framework, known for its rugged design, dependable performance, and high dispatch reliability.

Frequently Asked Questions

B737-300F Charter Queries

What is the payload capacity of the Boeing 737-300 Freighter?

The Boeing 737-300 Freighter has a maximum payload capacity of approximately 16,500 kg (16.5 metric tons), making it highly efficient for regional palletized cargo and express freight.

What is the maximum range of the B737-300F?

The aircraft has a maximum range of approximately 3,800 km (2,050 nautical miles), perfectly suited for short to medium-haul regional logistics and feeder routes.

How many pallets can the B737-300F accommodate?

The main deck can typically accommodate up to 8 standard 88-by-125-inch pallets, along with additional loose bulk cargo in the lower belly compartment.

What types of cargo is the B737-300F best suited for?

It is ideally suited for express parcel delivery, mail transport, e-commerce logistics, and general regional freight where operating efficiency is prioritized.
