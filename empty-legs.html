The Smart Way to Fly Private

What Is an Empty Leg Flight?

An empty leg flight is a private jet flight repositioning after a one-way charter — flying the same route, on the same aircraft, with the same crew, but without the original paying passenger on board. Instead of flying that leg empty, operators list it at a significant discount, giving travelers a rare chance to fly private for a fraction of the standard charter cost. The trade-off is flexibility: you take the route, date, and time the aircraft is already flying, rather than setting your own.

How Do I Find One?

The fastest way to catch an empty leg is to register your frequent routes with us — our concierge team alerts you directly by WhatsApp or email the moment a matching flight becomes available, often before it's listed publicly. You can also browse current availability by region below, or explore specific route pages for the corridors we cover most often.
