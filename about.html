About Skylinks Asia

An Islamabad-based charter brokerage connecting clients with vetted operators across Asia, the Middle East, and Africa. We operate strictly as your advocate and intermediary.

Our Story

Founded in 2015, Skylinks Asia Aviation began as a trusted word-of-mouth brokerage built on deep operational expertise and long-standing relationships within the aviation community.

Over the last 11 years, we have grown into a full-service charter broker, facilitating complex passenger, cargo, ACMI, and private jet requirements. We do not own aircraft; we leverage our established network of 20+ vetted operators to source the right aircraft for your specific mission, ensuring safety, compliance, and cost-effectiveness.

Our Brokerage Model

Transparency is our core principle. Skylinks Asia acts strictly as a facilitator between the charterer and the operator. We do not process payments, invoices, or handle funds. We charge a transparent, agreed-upon service fee/commission, ensuring no hidden margins or over-invoicing. The charterer pays the operator directly.

Vetted Operator Network

We only work with operators who maintain rigorous safety standards, valid AOCs, and comprehensive insurance.

Regional Expertise

Deep knowledge of overflight permits, ground handling, and regulatory landscapes across Asia, the Middle East, and Africa.
