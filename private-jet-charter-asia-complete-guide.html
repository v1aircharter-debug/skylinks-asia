What is private jet charter in Asia and how does it work?

Private jet charter in Asia is the on-demand hire of an entire aircraft — not a seat — for a specific itinerary. The charterer pays for the aircraft's block hours, not per passenger.

Private charter differs from scheduled business class in three ways: flexibility (departure time, airport, and route are set by the charterer), privacy (no shared cabin, no terminal queues), and direct routing (no connections through hub airports).

A charter broker like Skylinks sources aircraft from a network of operators who hold Air Operator Certificates in their home jurisdictions. The operator provides the aircraft, crew, maintenance, and insurance. The broker handles the enquiry, aircraft selection, permits, and coordination.

How much does a private jet charter in Asia cost?

Private jet charter in Asia costs $6,000 to $18,000 per flight hour depending on aircraft category, route, and season. A Singapore to Tokyo one-way in a midsize jet averages $50,000 to $65,000.

Cost drivers include aircraft category (light jets roughly $6,000–9,000/hr; midsize $9,000–13,000/hr; heavy $13,000–18,000/hr), route length and direction, permits and overflight fees, ground handling at origin and destination, crew duty time, and season.

What permits and regulations apply to private jet charter in Asia?

Private jet charter in Asia requires overflight and landing permits for each country crossed. Lead times vary from 24 hours for Singapore and Hong Kong to 7–10 days for China, Vietnam, and Myanmar.

Typical permit timelines by jurisdiction:

  • Singapore: 24–48 hours
  • Hong Kong: 24–48 hours
  • Japan: 3–5 business days
  • South Korea: 2–3 business days
  • Thailand: 48–72 hours
  • China: 7–10 business days
  • Vietnam: 5–7 business days
  • India: 3–5 business days
  • UAE: 24–72 hours

A charter broker handles permit applications on the operator's behalf, but the operator of record must hold the correct AOC and insurance for each jurisdiction.

How far in advance should you book a private jet charter in Asia?

Book intra-Asia private jet charters 48–72 hours ahead for the best availability. Cross-border flights into China or Vietnam need 7–10 days' notice; heavy jets during peak season should be booked 2–3 weeks out.

Peak seasons to plan around:

  • Q4 (October–December): year-end corporate travel, events
  • Chinese New Year (January–February): heavy demand in Greater China
  • Ramadan and Eid: Middle East–Asia family travel
  • Golden Week (October, May): Japan, China
