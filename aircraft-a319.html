Aircraft Overview

Efficiency Meets Premium Comfort

The Airbus A319 is a premier choice for medium-haul group charters, offering an exceptional balance of performance and passenger comfort. Accommodating up to 160 passengers, this versatile aircraft excels in applications like corporate MICE events, sports team transport, and VIP missions. With a robust 6,950 km range and 8.5-hour endurance, it connects international hubs efficiently. Its defining feature is the widest single-aisle cabin in its class, providing 18-inch-wide seats that ensure a premium, spacious travel experience. This makes the A319 ideal for clients seeking efficiency, reliability, and superior cabin comfort on every journey.

Frequently Asked Questions

Airbus A319 Charter Inquiries

How many passengers can the Airbus A319 hold?

The Airbus A319 can comfortably accommodate up to 160 passengers in a typical single-class charter configuration, making it ideal for medium-sized group travel, sports teams, and corporate MICE events.

What is the maximum range of a chartered Airbus A319?

The A319 has a maximum range of 6,950 kilometers (approximately 3,750 nautical miles), allowing for up to 8.5 hours of continuous flight. This makes it capable of connecting major hubs across Asia, the Middle East, and Europe without refueling.

Can the Airbus A319 be configured for VIP or executive travel?

Yes, the A319 is frequently converted into an Airbus Corporate Jet (ACJ). In a VIP configuration, the aircraft can be outfitted with luxury lounges, private bedrooms, and meeting rooms to accommodate around 20 to 50 VIP passengers.

What kind of runways does the A319 require for charter flights?

As a commercial narrowbody jet, the Airbus A319 requires paved, standardized runways typically at least 1,500 to 2,000 meters (5,000 to 6,500 feet) in length. It is not suited for unpaved or extremely short dirt strips, which would require a turboprop like an ATR 72 or Cessna Caravan.

How much baggage can the Airbus A319 carry?

The A319 offers generous belly cargo space in addition to large overhead bins inside the cabin. For a full flight of 160 passengers, it can typically accommodate standard checked luggage for all passengers. For groups with excess baggage or heavy equipment, the cargo hold can be configured to prioritize freight.
