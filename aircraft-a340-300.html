Overview

Aircraft Profile

The Airbus A340-300 is a premier long-haul charter solution, perfect for large corporate groups, sports travel, and religious pilgrimage flights. This intercontinental widebody airliner features a four-engine configuration, providing operational redundancy and peace of mind for global itineraries. Passengers benefit from a wonderfully quiet cabin environment and increased personal space within its twin-aisle design.

With a seating capacity reaching 440 in high-density layouts, it excels in efficiency. Performance highlights include a massive 13,240 km range and fifteen hour endurance. Whether utilized for automotive launches, music tours, or military troop movements, the A340-300 offers unparalleled versatility for specialized intercontinental aviation requirements.

Frequently Asked Questions

A340-300 Charter Queries

How many passengers can the Airbus A340-300 accommodate?

The Airbus A340-300 can accommodate up to 440 passengers in a high-density layout. Typical configurations range from 275 to 300 seats, offering high-density versatility by increasing capacity by up to 165 seats for large groups.

What is the maximum flight range and endurance of the A340-300?

The aircraft boasts a massive 13,240 km range with an impressive flight endurance of up to 15 hours, making it ideal for non-stop travel to distant international hubs.

Why is the A340-300 preferred for long-haul intercontinental travel?

It features a four-engine configuration that provides enhanced safety and operational redundancy for over-water flights. Additionally, it offers a "wonderfully quiet" widebody cabin environment designed to reduce passenger fatigue.

What is the cruise speed of the Airbus A340-300?

The Airbus A340-300 cruises at an efficient speed of 840 km/h, ensuring timely and reliable long-haul travel for both passengers and cargo.

What types of group travel is the A340-300 best suited for?

It is highly versatile and ideal for large corporate groups, sports travel, religious pilgrimage flights, automotive launches, music tours, and military troop movements.
