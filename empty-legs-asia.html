The Smart Way to Fly Private

What Is an Asia Empty Leg?

An empty leg flight is a private jet repositioning after a one-way charter — for example, a jet returning to Tokyo after dropping passengers in Seoul. Instead of flying that leg empty, operators list it at a significant discount, giving travelers a rare chance to fly private for a fraction of the standard charter cost. The trade-off is flexibility: you take the route, date, and time the aircraft is already flying, rather than setting your own.
