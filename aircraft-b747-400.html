Overview

Aircraft Profile

The Boeing 747-400, famously known as the "Jumbo Jet," remains a premier choice for high-capacity long-haul charter. As a four-engined, intercontinental aircraft, it delivers exceptional reliability for large-scale operations. Ideal for sports travel, pilgrimage charters, and cruise line exchanges, this wide-body airliner accommodates up to 580 passengers in high-density configurations.

Its impressive 14,200 km range and 15-hour endurance make it perfect for complex entertainment tours or global military movements. Whether utilized for corporate MICE events or seasonal tour operator series, the 747-400 offers unmatched seating capacity and performance, ensuring efficient aircraft leasing solutions for every diverse global industry.
