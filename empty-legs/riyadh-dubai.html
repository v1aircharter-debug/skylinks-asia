Riyadh to Dubai Empty Legs

Find current empty leg private jet flights between Riyadh and Dubai — up to 75% off standard charter rates. Updated regularly, subject to prior sale.

Home Empty Legs Middle East Riyadh–Dubai

Riyadh–Dubai is one of the busiest international private jet corridors in the Gulf, connecting Saudi Arabia's capital and center of government and corporate activity with the UAE's largest business and connecting hub. This is a two-way flow of charter traffic — corporate travel, government delegations, and onward international connections through Dubai — which makes it one of the more reliable routes for empty leg opportunities in the region. If you're looking for empty leg flights KSA to UAE, this corridor is where they appear most frequently.

Why This Route Regularly Produces Empty Legs

Dubai's role as a global connecting hub means aircraft frequently arrive from or depart to Riyadh as part of a longer international itinerary, not just as a point-to-point trip. Combined with Saudi Arabia's growing charter volume and Riyadh's status as the kingdom's primary government and corporate travel center, this corridor sees consistent two-way charter movement — and with it, a steady stream of repositioning flights in both directions.

Typical Aircraft on This Route

Riyadh–Dubai covers roughly 900 km, putting it well within range of most charter categories. Empty legs on this corridor are typically flown on:

  • Midsize to super-midsize jets for standard business travel
  • Heavy jets when repositioning onward to a longer international leg after dropping passengers in Dubai or Riyadh
  • Occasionally light jets for smaller, shorter-notice movements

Aircraft type varies flight to flight — check the current availability below for what's active right now.

How Much Can You Save?

As with other Gulf empty legs, Riyadh–Dubai flights typically list at 25–75% below the standard one-way charter rate for the same aircraft, with the exact discount depending on aircraft type and how close to departure the flight is listed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are there regular empty leg flights between Riyadh and Dubai?
Yes — as one of the busiest Gulf corridors, Riyadh–Dubai sees frequent charter movement in both directions, though specific dates and aircraft are never guaranteed in advance and are subject to prior sale.

How far in advance are Riyadh–Dubai empty legs listed?
Most are listed 48–72 hours before departure, sometimes same-day, once the operator confirms the original charter's schedule.

Midsize and super-midsize jets are most common, with heavy jets appearing when the aircraft is continuing on to a longer international route after this leg.
