Dubai Empty Leg FAQ

Dubai generates massive repositioning traffic. The most frequent empty leg routes connect Dubai to intra-Gulf hubs (Riyadh, Doha, Muscat), regional capitals (Almaty, Astana, Bukhara), and international leisure/financial centers (Paris, Mykonos, London).
Empty leg flights from Dubai typically list at 25-75% below the standard one-way charter rate for the same aircraft, depending on how close to departure the flight is booked.
Dubai sees a wide variety of aircraft for empty legs, ranging from Midsize jets like the Challenger 605 to Ultra Long Range jets like the Global 7500, Gulfstream G650ER, and even VIP widebodies like the Boeing 767 BBJ.
