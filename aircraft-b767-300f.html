Overview

Aircraft Profile

The Boeing 767-300 Freighter (767-300F) is a highly capable mid-size widebody cargo aircraft, widely recognized as the backbone of global express logistics. Designed specifically for cargo operations, it provides an unmatched balance of payload capacity, fuel efficiency, and reliability.

With the ability to carry over 52 metric tons of payload across 6,000 kilometers, the 767-300F is the preferred aircraft for global integrators like FedEx and UPS. Its main deck accommodates up to 24 standard pallets, while its lower belly offers superior containerized cargo volume. This makes it an incredibly efficient asset for e-commerce networks, automotive parts, and time-critical supply chain logistics.

Frequently Asked Questions

767-300F Charter Queries

What is the maximum payload capacity of the Boeing 767-300 Freighter?

The Boeing 767-300 Freighter has a maximum payload capacity of approximately 52,700 kg (52.7 metric tons or 116,000 pounds), making it highly efficient for mid-to-long-haul cargo routes.

How many pallets can the 767-300F carry on its main deck?

The main deck of the 767-300F can accommodate up to 24 standard AAA pallets (measuring 88 inches by 125 inches), along with additional containerized cargo in the lower belly.

What is the maximum range of the Boeing 767-300F freighter?

At maximum payload, the Boeing 767-300F has a range of approximately 6,020 kilometers (3,250 nautical miles), making it ideal for transatlantic and regional intercontinental logistics.

Why is the 767-300F so popular with express freight carriers like FedEx and Amazon?

The 767-300F offers an unmatched balance of payload capacity, fuel efficiency, and reliability. Its containerized cargo capacity and optimized operating economics make it the workhorse of choice for global express integrators.

Can the Boeing 767-300F carry temperature-controlled cargo?

Yes, the 767-300F can be equipped with active temperature control systems in its main deck and lower belly compartments, making it suitable for transporting pharmaceuticals, perishables, and other cold-chain goods.
