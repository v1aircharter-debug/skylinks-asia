Overview

Aircraft Profile

The Boeing 737-800BCF (Boeing Converted Freighter) is the industry standard for narrowbody cargo operations. Converted from the highly reliable Next-Generation 737-800 passenger jet, this aircraft provides exceptional fuel efficiency and lower operating costs for short and medium-haul logistics routes.

Featuring a large main deck cargo door and an advanced cargo handling system, the 737-800BCF can seamlessly accommodate up to 12 standard AAA pallets. It is the preferred choice for integrators, e-commerce giants, and postal services looking to move high-density express freight rapidly across regional networks.

Frequently Asked Questions

737-800BCF Charter Queries

What is the maximum payload capacity of the Boeing 737-800BCF?

The Boeing 737-800BCF has a maximum payload capacity of approximately 23,949 kg (23.9 metric tons or 52,800 pounds), making it highly efficient for dense, palletized cargo.

How many pallets can the 737-800BCF carry on its main deck?

The main deck of the 737-800BCF can accommodate up to 12 standard AAA pallets, which measure 88 inches by 125 inches, in addition to bulk cargo in the lower belly.

What is the maximum range of the Boeing 737-800BCF freighter?

At maximum payload, the Boeing 737-800BCF has a range of approximately 5,400 kilometers (2,900 nautical miles), allowing it to easily connect regional and medium-haul logistics hubs.

What type of cargo is the 737-800BCF best suited for?

It is the gold standard for e-commerce logistics, express parcel delivery, postal networks, perishables, and general commodity cargo on short to medium-haul routes.

How does the 737-800BCF compare to the older 737-400F?

The 737-800BCF offers nearly 20% more payload capacity, significantly better fuel efficiency, lower operating costs, and longer range compared to the legacy 737-400F.
